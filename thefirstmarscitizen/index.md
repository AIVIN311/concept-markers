# The First Mars Citizen

Series: civilization

Related concepts:

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how citizenship, identity, and
sovereignty may be redefined when humans become long-term residents beyond Earth.

The first Mars citizen is not primarily a prediction about who arrives first. It is a
marker for a civilizational threshold: the moment a human life becomes administratively
and socially anchored off-world.

In such conditions, familiar assumptions may weaken: birthplace as identity, territory as
jurisdiction, and Earth-based institutions as the default source of legitimacy.

This site does not advocate colonization, corporate settlement, or national expansion. It
does not provide timelines, mission updates, or operational claims.

Its purpose is to name a concept space where personhood, governance, and long-term
habitation intersect beyond a single planet.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **The First Mars Citizen**
has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視： 當人類在地球之外成為長期居民時，
公民、身份與主權如何可能被重新定義。

「第一位火星公民」首先並不是關於誰最先抵達。 它是一個文明門檻的標記：
某個時刻，人類生命在行政與社會意義上， 首次以「離地」作為其持續運作的基礎。

在這樣的條件下，許多既有假設可能開始鬆動： 出生地作為身份、領土作為管轄、
以及地球制度作為默認合法性的來源。

本網站不主張殖民、企業定居或國家擴張。 亦不提供時間表、任務更新或操作性斷言。

其目的是標示一個概念空間： 當人格、治理與長期居住首次跨出單一行星框架時， 它們將如何交會。

本頁刻意保持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**第一位火星公民（The First Mars Citizen）**
這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
