## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how allocation authority is
operationalized when distribution is systematized.

In this condition, control is exercised through quotas, queue design, priority tiers, and
access gates.

Power is less about ownership and more about release conditions: rate limits, schedulers,
cooldown windows, allowlists, denylists, and service refusal.

Allocation rules often appear not as policy language, but as system parameters and control
plane defaults.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a service, framework, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to name a governance condition in which allocation operates as
infrastructure.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Algorithmic Allocation Systems** has a stable
place to stand.
