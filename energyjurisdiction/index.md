## English

Energy jurisdiction is the practical authority to govern access to, allocation of, and
dependence on energy infrastructure across territories, markets, and technical systems.
It is not limited to formal legal, administrative, or institutional authority.

But energy infrastructure determines what can run, what can scale, and what must stop.

When power is scarce, priced, rationed, or interrupted, the grid becomes a governing
surface: allocation, priority, and denial become enforceable conditions.

**Energy Jurisdiction** does not presume a single policy framework.

It marks a structural force: energy systems increasingly function as jurisdictional
boundaries, even when no boundary is formally declared.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Energy Jurisdiction** has a stable place to
stand.

## 中文

「能源管轄權」是跨越領土、市場與技術系統，治理能源基礎設施之存取、分配與依賴關係的
實際權威。它不只限於正式的法律、行政或制度權威。

但能源基礎設施決定什麼能運行、什麼能擴張、以及什麼必須停止。

當電力稀缺、被定價、被配給或被中斷時，
電網會成為一個治理表面：分配、優先順序與拒絕會變成可被執行的條件。

「能源管轄權」不預設單一政策框架。

它標示的是一種結構力量：能源系統愈來愈像管轄邊界， 即使邊界從未被正式宣告。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**能源管轄權（Energy Jurisdiction）** 這個概念能有一個穩定的立足點。
