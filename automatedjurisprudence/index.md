# Automated Jurisprudence

Canonical URL: https://automatedjurisprudence.com/

Series: governance

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how legal reasoning and
jurisprudential operations are increasingly shaped, regulated, and executed by automated
systems — rather than being led solely by human judges, legislators, or legal
institutions.

Automated jurisprudence does not necessarily appear as the replacement of courts or law.

More often, it forms gradually through decision-support systems, automated enforcement
mechanisms, predictive legal models, risk assessment tools, and automated procedures
embedded within administrative workflows.

In such environments, legal outcomes may no longer arise primarily from interpretation and
deliberation, but are influenced by optimization rules, data thresholds, system defaults,
and computational procedures.

This site does not advocate a legal framework or policy position.

It does not provide legal advice or services.

Its purpose is to anchor a concept that is already operating in governance and legal
systems — one that often lacks clear boundaries, accountable responsibility, and a shared
vocabulary.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Automated Jurisprudence** has a stable place
to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：法律推理與法理運作如何愈來愈常由自動化系統所形塑、調節與執行，
而不再僅由人類法官、立法者或法律機構主導。

「自動化法理學」並不一定以取代法院或法律為形式出現。

它更常透過決策支援系統、自動執法機制、預測性法律模型、風險評估工具，
以及嵌入行政流程的自動化程序逐步形成。

在這類環境中，法律結果可能不再主要來自詮釋與審議，
而是受到最佳化規則、資料門檻、系統預設與計算流程所影響。

本站不主張任何法律框架或政策立場。

亦不提供法律建議或服務。

它的目的，是為一個已在治理與法制系統中實際運作的概念定錨——
這個概念往往缺乏清楚邊界、責任歸屬與共同語言。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**自動化法理學（Automated Jurisprudence）** 這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
