## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how personhood may emerge across
biological and synthetic systems.

Hybrid personhood does not assume a single substrate. It refers to conditions in which
identity, agency, and legal recognition are distributed between human, machine, or
institutional structures.

In such configurations, continuity and responsibility may no longer map cleanly onto one
body or one system.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a conceptual layer in which personhood becomes structurally hybrid
rather than singular.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Hybrid Personhood has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

在這樣的配置中，延續性與責任不再清楚對應於單一身體或單一系統。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供框架、服務或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一個概念層：人格從單一形態轉為結構性混合。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「混合人格（Hybrid Personhood）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
