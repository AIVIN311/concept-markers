# The Pacific Pivot

Canonical URL: https://thepacificpivot.com/

Series: civilization

Related concepts:

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining the structural shift of economic,
technological, and geopolitical gravity toward the Pacific region.

The Pacific pivot does not occur through a single policy announcement or alliance. It
emerges through trade flows, supply-chain realignment, technological concentration,
military positioning, and demographic momentum.

Over time, institutions, capital, and influence increasingly orient around
Pacific-centered networks rather than Atlantic ones.

This reorientation can reshape dependency, strategic access, institutional priorities, and
which regions set the operating assumptions of global systems.

This site does not interpret the shift as opportunity or threat. It does not promote
strategic doctrines or political agendas.

Its purpose is to mark a long-term civilizational reorientation already unfolding across
global systems.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **The Pacific Pivot**
has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視： 經濟、科技與地緣政治重心如何逐步轉向太平洋區域。

「太平洋轉向」並非透過單一政策宣告或聯盟形成。 它透過貿易流動、供應鏈重組、科技集中、
軍事佈署與人口動能逐漸浮現。

隨著時間推移，制度、資本與影響力越來越圍繞
以太平洋為核心的網絡運作，而非傳統的大西洋體系。

這種重心轉移可能重塑依賴、戰略存取、制度優先順序，以及哪些區域能設定全球系統的運作前提。

本網站不將此轉變解讀為機會或威脅。 亦不推廣任何戰略學說或政治立場。

其目的是標示一個已在全球系統中展開的 長期文明重心轉移。

本頁刻意保持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保 **太平洋轉向（The Pacific Pivot）**
這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
