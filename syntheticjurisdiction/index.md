# Synthetic Jurisdiction

Canonical URL: https://syntheticjurisdiction.com/

Series: synthetic_governance

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining jurisdiction in synthetic
environments.

Synthetic systems generate content, signals, identities, and behaviors that can act in the
real world without being tied to a single location, authority, or accountable origin.

As synthetic data and synthetic reality scale, questions of responsibility become
jurisdictional: who can enforce, who can audit, and who must answer.

Synthetic jurisdiction does not assume a single legal regime or a universal policy
framework.

It marks the necessity of enforceable boundaries where synthetic outputs are treated as
actionable, governable, and attributable.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Synthetic Jurisdiction** has a stable place to
stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：合成環境中的「管轄」問題。

合成系統會產生內容、訊號、身分與行為， 這些輸出能在現實世界產生影響，
卻往往不隸屬於單一地點、單一權威或可追溯的責任來源。

當合成資料與合成現實規模化後， 「責任」會變成一個管轄問題：
誰能執行？誰能稽核？誰必須回應？

「合成管轄權」不預設單一法律體系，也不主張一套普世政策框架。

它標示的是一個必要性： 合成輸出必須被視為可被處理、可被治理、可被歸屬的對象，
否則影響會存在，但責任永遠找不到落點。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**合成管轄權（Synthetic Jurisdiction）** 這個概念能有一個穩定的立足點。
