# Unannounced Sovereignty

Canonical URL: https://unannouncedsovereignty.com/

Series: governance

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how authority can be exercised
without formal announcement.

Unannounced sovereignty does not arise through constitutions, treaties, or public
authorization. It emerges through control over infrastructure, standards, interfaces, and
operational dependency.

In such conditions, power is real and effective, even when it is never explicitly named as
sovereignty.

This site does not assign legitimacy or illegitimacy.

Its purpose is to mark a condition of governance: authority operating without
announcement, representation, or symbolic recognition.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Unannounced Sovereignty**
has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為檢視權威的參考點：它可以在無需正式公告的情況下被行使。

「未宣布的主權」並非透過憲法、條約或公共授權而來。
它透過對基礎設施、標準、介面與操作依賴的控制而產生。

在這種情況下，權力是真實且有效的， 即使它從未明確被稱為主權。

本網站不賦予任何合法性或非法性。

其目的是標示一種治理存在的條件： 權威在沒有公告、沒有代表性或象徵性認可的情況下運作。

本頁刻意保持簡約。

它的存在，是為了確保 **未宣布的主權（Unannounced Sovereignty）**
這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
