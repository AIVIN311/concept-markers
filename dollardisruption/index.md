# Dollar Disruption

Canonical URL: https://dollardisruption.com/

Series: civilization

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how shifts in monetary
infrastructure, trust, and settlement regimes can destabilize the dollar order without
formal replacement.

Dollar disruption does not require a single challenger currency. It may emerge through
alternative settlement channels, liquidity fragmentation, sanctions-driven rerouting,
regional payment networks, or changes in collateral trust.

In such conditions, dominance can erode by degrees: not through declaration, but through
migration of defaults and routing choices.

That migration can redistribute liquidity, settlement risk, bargaining power, and the
institutions relied upon to coordinate cross-border exchange.

This site does not advocate a monetary thesis, investment strategy, or policy program. It
does not provide forecasts or recommendations.

Its purpose is to name a structural phenomenon: the dollar order experiencing disruption
as an infrastructural and governance shift.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Dollar Disruption**
has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：貨幣基礎設施、信任與清算體系的變化，
如何在不出現「正式取代者」的情況下動搖美元秩序。

「美元動盪」不一定需要一個單一的挑戰貨幣。
它可能透過替代清算通道、流動性碎片化、制裁導致的路由改道、
區域支付網路，或抵押品信任的變化而逐步浮現。

在這樣的條件下，主導地位可能以「漸進」的方式被削弱：
不是透過宣告，而是透過預設值與路由選擇的遷移。

這種遷移可能重新分配流動性、清算風險、議價權，以及跨境交換所依賴的協調制度。

本網站不主張任何貨幣論述、投資策略或政策計畫。 亦不提供預測或建議。

其目的是為一個結構性現象命名： 美元秩序作為基礎設施與治理結構，正在經歷動盪與重排。

本頁刻意保持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保 **美元動盪（Dollar Disruption）**
這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
