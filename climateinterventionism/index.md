# Climate Interventionism

Canonical URL: https://climateinterventionism.com/

Series: civilization

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining deliberate actions taken to alter
climatic systems at scale.

Climate interventionism does not necessarily begin as a unified program.

It may emerge through experiments, emergency measures, or localized responses that
gradually extend beyond their original scope.

As intervention becomes normalized, the distinction between mitigation, adaptation, and
control may blur.

This site does not advocate for or against intervention.

Its purpose is to mark a conceptual space where technological capability, ecological
uncertainty, and governance intersect.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Climate Interventionism** has a stable place
to stand.

本站不主張支持或反對干預行動。

它的目的，是標示一個概念空間： 當技術能力、生態不確定性與治理開始交會時，
這些行動如何被理解與運作。

**氣候干預主義（Climate Interventionism）** 這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
