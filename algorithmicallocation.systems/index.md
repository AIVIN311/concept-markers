## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how allocation authority is
operationalized when distribution is systematized.

Algorithmic allocation systems are not defined by automated distribution alone. The
structural condition appears when system operators can determine who receives access, in
what order, and under which constraints.

Control is exercised through quotas, queue design, priority tiers, access gates, rate
limits, schedulers, cooldown windows, allowlists, denylists, and service refusal.

These mechanisms turn allocation rules into operating conditions. What appears as a system
parameter or control-plane default can shape access, dependency, and institutional
accountability.

This site does not advocate a position or provide a service, framework, or policy
proposal.

Its purpose is to name a governance condition in which allocation operates as
infrastructure.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Algorithmic Allocation Systems** has a stable
place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：當分配被系統化後，分配權力如何被轉化為可執行的控制條件。

演算法分配系統不只指自動化分配。當系統營運者能決定誰可取得資源、取得順序與適用限制時，
這種結構性條件才會形成。

控制透過配額、佇列設計、優先級、存取閘門、速率限制、排程器、冷卻時間、允許名單、拒絕名單與服務拒絕被執行。

這些機制把分配規則轉化為運作條件。看似中性的系統參數或控制平面預設，可能重塑存取、依賴與制度責任。

本站不主張任何立場，亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

它的目的，是為「分配作為基礎設施」的治理現象命名。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**演算法分配系統層（Algorithmic Allocation Systems）**
這個概念能有一個穩定的立足點。
