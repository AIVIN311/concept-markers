## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how personhood may be attributed to
non-biological systems.

Synthetic personhood concerns recognition structures in which agency, representation, or
responsibility are extended beyond human embodiment.

In such environments, identity may be assigned through formal criteria rather than organic
continuity.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a conceptual layer where personhood becomes decoupled from
biological origin.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Synthetic Personhood has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：人格如何可能被歸屬於非生物系統。

合成人格關注的是：在某些承認結構中，行動能力、代表性或責任如何延伸至人類身體之外。

在這類環境中，身分可能依據形式準則被指派，而非依賴有機延續。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一個概念層：人格與生物來源之間開始解耦。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「合成人格（Synthetic Personhood）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
