## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how resources are increasingly
allocated by automated systems rather than negotiated by institutions or markets alone.

Algorithmic allocation does not require visible commands or public decisions. It emerges
through prioritization rules, optimization models, queue systems, eligibility criteria,
and real-time access controls.

Energy, computing power, credit, bandwidth, housing, healthcare, and attention are
progressively distributed by systems that rank, throttle, approve, or deny in real time.

In such environments, power is exercised not by owning resources directly, but by
controlling the mechanisms that assign them.

This site does not advocate a position or provide a service, framework, or policy
proposal.

Its purpose is to name a structural shift already operating across infrastructure,
governance, and economic systems — often without explicit recognition.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Algorithmic Allocation** has a stable place to
stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：資源如何愈來愈由自動化系統分配，
而非僅由制度協商或市場交換決定。

演算法分配不需要透過可見命令或公開決策運作；它透過優先順序規則、最佳化模型、佇列系統、資格門檻與即時存取控制逐步形成。

能源、算力、資金、頻寬、住房、醫療與注意力， 正愈來愈被即時排序、限制、核准或拒絕。

在這樣的環境中，權力不再主要來自擁有資源本身， 而是來自控制分配機制。

本站不主張任何立場，亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

它的目的，是為一個已在基礎設施、治理與經濟系統中運作的轉變命名，
而這個轉變往往尚未被清楚意識或具備詞彙。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**演算法分配權（Algorithmic Allocation）** 這個概念能有一個穩定的立足點。
