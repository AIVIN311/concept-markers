# Offworld Sovereignty

Canonical URL: https://offworldsovereignty.com/

Series: offworld_governance

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how sovereignty may be redefined
when governance extends beyond Earth-based territory.

In offworld environments, land, borders, and citizenship no longer function as primary
sources of political authority.

Governance may instead emerge from control over life-support systems, resource
infrastructures, automated decision systems, and shared operational protocols.

Offworld sovereignty does not assume nation-state dominance, nor does it prescribe legal
or political outcomes.

It marks a condition in which legitimacy, jurisdiction, and authority must be
reconstructed under non-terrestrial constraints.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Offworld Sovereignty** has a stable place to
stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視： 當治理行為脫離地球疆界後，主權將如何被重新定義。

在地外環境中，土地、邊界與國籍不再是政治權力的主要來源。

治理權力可能轉而建立於 生命維持系統、資源基礎設施、自動化決策系統， 以及共同運作協議之上。

「地外主權」不預設國家主導， 也不提出具體的法律或政治方案。

它標示的是一種狀態： 在非地球條件下，正當性、管轄權與權威必須被重新構築。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保 **地外主權（Offworld Sovereignty）**
這個概念能有一個穩定的立足點。
