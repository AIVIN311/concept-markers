## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how allocation authority is generated when distribution depends on model inference and real-time scoring.

In this condition, eligibility is no longer a fixed rule; it is inferred from features, risk profiles, and behavioral signals.

Queues and priorities are not merely administrative order. They are continuously recalculated by models that re-rank who is seen as admissible.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a service, framework, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a governance condition in which allocation power increasingly exists in inferential form.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Algorithmic Allocation AI** has a stable place to stand.
