## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how jurisdiction may operate within
orbital infrastructures.

Orbital jurisdiction refers to the regulatory, administrative, and sovereign reach applied
to satellites, stations, and space-based systems.

As orbital layers become economically and strategically significant, jurisdiction may be
distributed across states, corporations, and treaty regimes.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a structural condition in which governance extends into orbital
layers.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Orbital Jurisdiction has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：管轄如何在軌道基礎設施內運作。

軌道管轄指的是：適用於衛星、太空站與各類太空系統的監管、行政與主權可及範圍。

隨著軌道層在經濟與戰略上日益關鍵，管轄可能分布於國家、企業與條約制度之間。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一種結構條件：治理已延伸進入軌道層。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「軌道管轄（Orbital Jurisdiction）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
