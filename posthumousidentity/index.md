# Posthumous Identity

Canonical URL: https://posthumousidentity.com/

Series: identity

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how identity persists, transforms,
or is reconstructed after biological death.

Posthumous identity may emerge through digital records, archived communications, biometric
remnants, simulations, or representations generated by others or by systems.

In such cases, identity is no longer anchored to agency or consent, yet continues to
operate within social, legal, or computational contexts.

Decisions about custody, representation, and reuse can therefore shape memory, legal
claims, and the authority to act in the name of someone who can no longer respond.

This site does not advocate preservation, deletion, or replication of identity.

Its purpose is to mark a conceptual space where technology, memory, representation, and
authority intersect beyond the lifespan of the individual.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Posthumous Identity** has a stable place to
stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：身分如何在生物死亡後持續存在、轉化，或被重建。

身後身分可能透過數位紀錄、存檔通訊、生物特徵殘留物、模擬，
或由他人／系統所產生的表徵而浮現。

在這種情況下，身分不再依賴行動力或同意， 卻仍持續在社會、法律或計算脈絡中運作。

因此，關於保管、再現與再利用的決定，可能形塑記憶、法律主張，以及誰能以無法再回應之人的名義行動。

本站不主張保留、刪除或複製身分。

它的目的，是標示一個概念空間：當科技、記憶、代表性與權威
開始超越個人壽命時，它們如何交會、如何運作。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**身後身分（Posthumous Identity）** 這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
