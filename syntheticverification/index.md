## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how verification may be performed
through automated systems.

Synthetic verification refers to processes in which identity, authenticity, or compliance
are validated through machine logic and procedural checks.

As verification pipelines scale, trust may be instantiated through reproducible validation
rather than interpersonal assurance.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a structural condition in which verification is system-mediated.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Synthetic Verification has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

隨著驗證管線擴張，信任可能透過可重現的驗證程序被具體化，而非依賴人際保證。
