## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how civilizations store, accumulate,
and reuse structures, knowledge, and systems over time.

Civilization caching does not occur only through archives or libraries.

It emerges through institutions, infrastructures, legal frameworks, technological
platforms, cultural practices, and inherited systems that persist beyond their original
context.

In such processes, past decisions, designs, and values remain embedded, shaping future
possibilities and constraints.

This site does not propose models of preservation or reform.

Its purpose is to mark a structural phenomenon in which civilizations operate not only
through innovation, but through accumulated layers of cached systems.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Civilization Caching**
has a stable place to stand.

其目的是標示一種結構性現象： 文明的運作不僅依賴創新， 也依賴累積層疊的緩存系統。

本頁刻意保持簡約。
