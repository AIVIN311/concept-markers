## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how certification may be issued
through automated or synthetic systems.

Synthetic certification refers to credentials granted by procedural pipelines rather than
by direct human evaluation.

As compliance and qualification become encoded into machine-readable criteria, recognition
may be produced through validation logic and rule execution.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a structural condition in which certification becomes
system-generated rather than individually adjudicated.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Synthetic Certification has a stable place to stand.
