## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how citizenship may be defined or
registered through digital and automated systems.

Synthetic citizenship concerns forms of civic status instantiated through databases,
identity layers, and jurisdictional protocols.

In such conditions, belonging may be recorded, verified, and managed through
infrastructure rather than solely through territorial residence.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a structural shift in how civic membership is constituted.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Synthetic Citizenship has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：公民資格如何可能透過數位與自動化系統被定義或登錄。

合成公民關注的是：公民身分如何透過資料庫、身分層與管轄協議被具體化。

在這樣的條件下，歸屬可能透過基礎設施被記錄、驗證與管理，而不僅僅依賴領土居住。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一種結構轉變：公民會員關係如何被構成。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「合成公民（Synthetic Citizenship）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
