# Silicon Metabolism

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how digital and AI infrastructures
consume energy, resources, and environmental inputs in patterns resembling biological
metabolic systems.

Silicon metabolism does not refer to individual devices or data centers alone.

It emerges through global computing networks, large-scale AI training, cloud
infrastructures, cooling systems, and resource extraction.

In such systems, technological growth becomes tied to continuous material consumption and
environmental impact.

This site does not promote sustainability strategies or energy solutions. It does not
provide efficiency metrics.

Its purpose is to mark a systemic process already unfolding across technological
development and planetary resource use.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Silicon Metabolism** has a stable place to
stand.

本站不推廣永續策略或能源解決方案， 亦不提供效率指標。

它的目的，是標示一個已在科技發展與行星資源利用中展開的系統性過程。
