## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how personhood may be defined beyond
terrestrial jurisdiction.

Offworld personhood concerns conditions in which identity and legal status are extended
into orbital, lunar, or other extra-planetary environments.

When habitation and governance move beyond Earth, the assumptions underlying citizenship,
rights, and representation may require structural reinterpretation.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a conceptual space where personhood intersects with offworld
sovereignty and jurisdictional design.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Offworld Personhood has a stable place to stand.

當居住與治理超出地球範圍時，公民身分、權利與代表權背後的前提可能需要結構性重釋。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一個概念空間：人格在此與域外主權及管轄設計相互交會。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「域外人格（Offworld Personhood）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
