## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how accountability may be structured
at the protocol level.

Protocol accountability concerns systems in which rules are embedded in code and enforced
automatically rather than through discretionary authority.

In such environments, responsibility may be diffused across developers, operators, and
network participants.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a structural shift in how accountability is instantiated within
distributed systems.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Protocol Accountability has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：責任如何在協議層被結構化。

協議責任關注的是：當規則被嵌入程式碼並由系統自動執行時，責任如何被建立，而非依賴裁量性權威。

在這類環境中，責任可能分散於開發者、運維者與網路參與者之間。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一種結構轉變：責任如何在分散式系統內被具體化。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「協議責任層（Protocol Accountability）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
