## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how attestation may be generated by
synthetic or automated systems.

Synthetic attestation refers to proofs, validations, or credentials issued by machine
processes rather than direct human assertion.

As verification pipelines become automated, trust may increasingly rely on cryptographic
and procedural validation.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a condition in which attestation is produced through system-level
processes.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Synthetic Attestation has a stable place to stand.
