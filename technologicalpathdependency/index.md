# Technological Path Dependency

Canonical URL: https://technologicalpathdependency.com/

Series: synthetic_systems

Related concepts:

- none

## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how early technological choices
constrain or shape future possibilities.

Technological path dependency does not require optimal design. It emerges through
adoption, standardization, infrastructure investment, and compatibility requirements that
favor continuity over reconsideration.

As systems scale, reversal becomes increasingly costly, even when superior alternatives
exist.

The resulting dependency shapes procurement, skills, governance, and institutional
expectations, narrowing which alternatives remain practical rather than merely possible.

This site does not promote innovation strategies or intervention models.

Its purpose is to name a structural dynamic that operates across platforms, institutions,
and societies — often perceived as inevitability rather than design.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Technological Path Dependency** has a stable
place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：早期的技術選擇如何限制或形塑未來可能性。

「技術路徑依賴」不需要最佳化設計才會形成。
它透過採用、標準化、基礎設施投資與相容性要求而浮現，
使系統傾向延續既有路徑，而非重新評估。

隨著系統規模擴大，回頭與翻轉會變得越來越昂貴—— 即使存在更優的替代方案。

由此形成的依賴會形塑採購、技能、治理與制度預期，讓替代方案即使仍然可能，也逐漸失去實際可行性。

本站不推廣創新策略或介入模型。

它的目的，是為一種跨平台、制度與社會運作的結構性動力命名——
而它往往被感受為「不可避免」，而非「可被設計」。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**技術路徑依賴（Technological Path Dependency）**
這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
