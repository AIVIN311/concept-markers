## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how legal entities may be
constituted through automated or procedural systems.

Synthetic legal entity refers to juridical persons instantiated via registration logic,
smart contracts, or algorithmic incorporation processes.

As formation becomes automated, responsibility and liability may be structured through
code-mediated frameworks.

This site does not advocate a position.

It does not provide a framework, service, or policy proposal.

Its purpose is to mark a condition in which legal personality is procedurally generated.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term Synthetic Legal Entity has a stable place to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：法律主體如何可能透過自動化或程序系統被構成。

合成法律主體指的是：法人資格透過註冊邏輯、智能合約或演算法設立流程而被具體化。

當設立流程自動化，責任與法律義務可能透過程式中介框架被結構化。

本站不主張任何立場。

亦不提供服務、框架或政策提案。

其目的，是標示一種條件：法律人格由程序性機制生成。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保「合成法律主體（Synthetic Legal Entity）」這個詞有一個穩定的立足點。
