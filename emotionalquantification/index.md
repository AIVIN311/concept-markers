## English

This domain serves as a reference point for examining how human emotions are increasingly
measured, modeled, predicted, and transformed into data-driven variables.

Emotional quantification does not arise only through psychological research or wearable
technologies.

It emerges through sentiment analysis, behavioral prediction, biometric monitoring,
engagement optimization, and algorithmic profiling.

In such systems, feelings become metrics, experiences become datasets, and emotional
states become inputs for automated decision-making.

This site does not promote mental health tools or analytic platforms. It does not provide
assessments or services.

Its purpose is to mark a systemic transformation already unfolding across technology,
commerce, governance, and human interaction.

This page is intentionally minimal.

It exists to ensure the term **Emotional Quantification** has a stable place
to stand.

## 中文

本網域作為一個參考標記，用於檢視：人類情感如何日益被衡量、建模、預測，
並轉換成數據驅動的變數。

「情感量化」並非僅透過心理學研究或穿戴式科技產生。

它透過情感分析、行為預測、生物特徵監控、互動優化與演算法側寫等方式浮現。

在這樣的系統中，感覺成為指標，經驗成為資料集， 情緒狀態成為自動化決策的輸入。

本站不推廣心理健康工具或分析平台， 亦不提供評估或服務。

它的目的，是標示一場已在科技、商業、治理與人際互動中展開的系統性轉型。

本頁刻意維持極簡。

它存在，是為了確保
**情感量化（Emotional Quantification）** 這個詞能有一個穩定的立足點。
